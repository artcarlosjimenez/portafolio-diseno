Motion Designer · Art Director

I'm a Motion Designer and Art Director based in Guatemala City, currently leading motion graphics and visual identity work at Studio Dosis. Over the past thirteen years I've moved between animation studios, agencies and the classroom — directing 2D animation, building visual identities, and handling compositing on productions that had to hold up on web, television and everywhere in between.

I like projects where craft and pipeline meet: a character that needs to read at frame one, a brand system that survives contact with a hundred formats, an FX pass that sells the whole shot. That's the work I keep coming back for.
